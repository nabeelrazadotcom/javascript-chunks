Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about visa applications, required documents, processing times, and how we can help.

  • 1. What types of visas do you assist with?

    We assist with tourist, student, work, business, and family visit visas for various destinations worldwide.

  • 2. Which documents are required for a visa application?

    Requirements vary by country and visa type. Common documents include a valid passport, photographs, financial statements, and supporting documents.

  • 3. How long does the visa process take?

    Processing times depend on the destination, visa category, and embassy workload. We help you understand the expected timeline for your application.

  • 4. Can you guarantee visa approval?

    No consultant can guarantee approval. The final decision rests with the relevant embassy or immigration authority.

  • 5. How much does a visa application cost?

    The total cost depends on the visa category, destination, government fees, and consultancy services required.

  • 6. Can I apply for a visa if I have been rejected before?

    Yes, in many cases. We can review your previous refusal, identify potential issues, and help you prepare a stronger application where appropriate.

  • 7. Do you help with visa interview preparation?

    Yes. We provide guidance on common interview questions, required documents, and how to communicate your travel purpose clearly and honestly.

  • 8. Can I track the status of my visa application?

    Tracking options depend on the destination and application process. We can guide you through the available status-checking methods.

  • 9. Do you provide student visa consultancy?

    Yes. We assist students with visa documentation, financial evidence, admission-related requirements, and interview preparation, depending on the destination.

  • 10. What should I do if my visa application is delayed?

    Check the official application status and any published processing updates. We can help you understand the available follow-up options.
